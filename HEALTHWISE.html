HEALTHWISE is an educational website created to help students learn more about taking care of their health.

It covers important topics such as physical and mental health, nutrition, hygiene, healthy lifestyle choices, and warning signs that may require medical attention.

Nutrition

A balanced diet provides the nutrients your body needs to grow, function, and stay healthy.

Carbohydrates provide energy for everyday activities. Examples include rice, bread, potatoes, and grains.

Protein helps build and repair body tissues. Examples include fish, eggs, meat, beans, and tofu.

Vegetables provide vitamins, minerals, and fiber that support overall health.

Fruits provide vitamins, minerals, fiber, and other nutrients that support a healthy diet.

Water helps the body regulate temperature and supports many important body functions.

A balanced meal includes a variety of foods that provide different nutrients.

Hygiene & Prevention

Wash your hands regularly to help remove germs and reduce the spread of illness.

🦷

Oral Hygiene

Personal Hygiene

Regular bathing and clean clothes can help maintain personal cleanliness.

Cover Coughs

Cover your coughs and sneezes to help reduce the spread of respiratory germs.
